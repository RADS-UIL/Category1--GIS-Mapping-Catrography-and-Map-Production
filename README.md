## 1. DKT Karachi Clinic Catchments

**Population and Built-Environment Catchments of DKT Clinics, Karachi**

> Location: Karachi, Pakistan

500 m catchments around 63 DKT provider clinics, split into NE/SE/SW/NW quadrants and cut where neighbouring circles meet so nothing is counted twice. Each catchment is profiled with buildings, points of interest, and 2023 population (total, women of reproductive age, under-1/2/5 by sex).

**Key results**

- 63 providers became 57 non-overlapping catchments in five Karachi districts (Malir 27, East 15, Korangi 12, West 6, Central 3 providers).
- 197,794 buildings and 2,544 OSM points of interest were mapped.
- About 1.06M people fall in the catchments, including about 170K MWRA and about 143K children under 5. The largest single catchment holds about 69.7K people.

## 2. Health Facility Catchment Population (World Bank)

**Health Facility Catchment Population, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa**

> Client / Donor: World Bank · Location: Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan

An interactive map of 1,818 facilities (BHU, RHC, CHC, THQ, DHQ, hospitals) in 21 districts. It estimates how many people live within a 5 km radius, a walking radius and a vehicle radius, with each person counted once.

**Key results**

- 23.9M people are covered. 90.9% (21.7M) live within 5 km of a facility.
- 88.1% (21.1M) are within the walking radius of 4.5 km, based on 4.5 km/h.
- 100% are within the vehicle radius of 30 km, based on 30 km/h. District and tehsil boundary layers are included.
